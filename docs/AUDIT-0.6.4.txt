Version 0.6.4 - proportional patrols and free cruise versus pursuit

User clarification supersedes 0.6.3's blanket unknown-equipment block:
small unknown groups may be attacked; large groups need equipment scouting.
Radiating scoop warp 6 is free routine movement, not a maximum pursuit speed.
Investigate fifteen destroyers assigned against three weak ships.

Changes:
- An uncertain encounter of eight or more reported ships requires a scout.
  Fewer remain eligible, with the existing strength, fresh-intel, fuel, return
  and mine safety checks. Nearby and visibly converging reported contacts count
  together. Missing target ship counts remain a scouting requirement. The rule
  applies to saved jobs, target selection, retargeting and final departure.
- Against a small unarmed encounter, a previously merged patrol can release
  excess ships. A deterministic subset must meet assessed power and desired
  weapon count, have a funded attack/return, and leave the surplus enough fuel
  to return. Native splitting conserves ships, designs and fuel. The new patrol
  receives no inherited attack job and can be assigned other duties.
- Do not reduce groups against armed encounters or visible large reinforcement
  groups using this weak-target shortcut. Cargo, damage, borrowed escort support,
  maintenance and reserved fuel missions remain protected.
- Fuel revalidation recognizes an approved patrol attack as a fast funded
  mission. It can use safe engine speeds above warp 6. Routine containment and
  free radiating-scoop patrol still use economical warp 6.
