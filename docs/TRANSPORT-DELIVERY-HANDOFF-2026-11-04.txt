Changes

* An already loaded passenger delivery keeps its usable receiver despite competing
  reservations. It can recover after six full years without getting closer, or
  choose another receiver if ownership/capacity makes the old one unusable.
* Orphaned passenger/installation cargo regains a delivery. Loaded freighters defer
  new refits even when an old planning tag disappeared. Existing active refits and
  funded replacement orders retain the 0.6.12 safeguards.
* Packed installations follow the actual passenger receiver. Exports remain
  opportunistic and retain at least 95% of current operating installation capacity
  after the actual mixed passenger/mineral/building load, not the proposed load.
* New passenger pickups give fragile colonies below 10% population precedence over
  habitability among otherwise comparable full, unreserved, safe deliveries.
* Fuel replanning replaces both the final coordinates and target identity. A saved
  intermediate dock ID must not overwrite the final passenger/invasion destination.
* Mineral cargo can reach its assigned own colony without a starbase.
* All existing bases target at least 500 kT Ironium and 500 kT Germanium. Existing
  shipyard Boranium reserve stays 300 kT; other bases gain no Boranium floor.
  Existing incoming-load reservations and donor production reserves apply.

Interpretation and limits

Snapshot cargo decreases are lower bounds on actual transfers, not exact manifests.
Early copied turns show installation receiver mismatches falling to zero and actual
installation/mineral unloading. Colony losses and escort/fuel constraints remain
strategic conditions; this does not claim that every loaded ship can safely sail.
No new successful invasion occurred in the eight-turn copied campaign. The
long-standing hostile target in the private audit has severely stale population
reports and subsequently a known base; sending unarmed transports regardless would
not be a valid repair. Native capture is independently tested on an eligible target.
No waiting in hostile orbit, invented intelligence, relaxed safety, free cargo,
partial-load departure, or independent installation-hauling mission was introduced.
