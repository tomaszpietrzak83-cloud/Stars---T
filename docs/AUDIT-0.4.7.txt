Requests 240-244
- Visible short explanations accompany new-game map settings and flags.
- Edge placement minimizes distance to the 5% inset perimeter, normalized
  independently for rectangular width/height. Both initial selection and the
  final assignment of the human capital use the same metric. Center is retained.
- Penetrating scouts greedily choose shared scan stops, weighing covered worlds
  against travel years/fuel. Candidate areas include nearest approaches,
  midpoints and intersecting scanner circles. This is a bounded heuristic,
  not a claim of a globally optimal itinerary. Counts refer to stop positions;
  scanners do not continuously discover planets along movement segments.
  Complete displayed expeditions include a fuel-funded return. Targets are
  reserved between scouts; ordinary scanners retain orbital exploration.
- Marked enemy colonies receive refresh scans first. Other stale enemy colonies
  can be refreshed after discovery work is exhausted. Nearby colonies share a
  scan position when useful. Incoming fleets use public reported motion and
  cloak-adjusted normal/penetrating ranges, with shared areas for nearby reports.
  Unknown equipment still requires contact unless racial discovery-on-scan
  permits remote identification. No hidden orders or physical enemy data are
  used by route selection. Actual native scanning grants observations.
- Command sections collapse independently, persist in browser localStorage,
  and retain headers/actions. Dynamic overview/base/location titles use stable
  section keys. Initially expanded; unavailable storage does not break controls.
- VictoryConditions now follows the parent settings object. The former local
  initial copy overwrote restored preferences and failed to follow resets.
  Saved host/AI race snapshots remain selectable even if absent from the current
  race library. Preferences remain local to the browser and website origin.
