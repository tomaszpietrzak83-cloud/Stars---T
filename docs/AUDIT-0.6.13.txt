tomek-stars 0.6.13 — global production, fleet selection and completed deliveries
2026-11-04

Changes
- Commands > Ship Production opens a separate empire-wide production screen on
  desktop and phone. Select a current own design, total quantity and new ships'
  Auto orders; Fill this year's capacity chooses the real aggregate free capacity.
  Existing paid/concrete work stays first. Native mining, queue, production budget,
  research and dock mass limits are used on private copies. All available current
  year capacity is filled before later batches are allocated by completion year.
  Green and blue batches stay separate; the preview shows both totals per base.
- A queue position is green only when its ENTIRE batch finishes within one year.
  Increasing the quantity beyond that deadline turns the item blue even when its
  first ship would still finish this year. This corrects the 0.6.12 interpretation.
- The chosen role belongs to the particular production queue item. New fleets
  receive it through actual native construction; explicit Manual overrides a
  design default. Existing fleets/design defaults and old paid orders are not
  changed. Partial batches retain the override for subsequent construction.
- Production preview is read-only. Applying verifies the source hash/year/state,
  recalculates and saves all bases in a transaction; a stale/repeated request is
  rejected and a failed second-yard save rolls everything back. UI ignores stale
  previews and blocks applying pending/read-only orders.
- Automation supports real Shift-click range selection in displayed order,
  selection by fleet name and bulk Auto orders with an explicit eligible count.
  Equipment, dedicated minesweeper, active maintenance/assembly restrictions and
  read-only controls remain. One player-order save; failures restore old roles.
- Merged transport branches d43fe2e and 2de0a7c9 preserve loaded deliveries before
  new refits, destination identity/reservations and actual mixed-load operating
  capacity; recover orphaned deliveries; deliver to colonies without bases;
  prefer fragile colonies and existing funded safe dock detours when escorts are
  optional. Mandatory escort minimum, fuel, mine and combat guards remain.
  Bases now keep/receive at least 500 kT Ironium and Germanium; Boranium unchanged.
- Yellow ship-loss damage marks stay inside the bottom battle-board row at large
  display scale. Locale-sensitive tests retain existing localized game numbers.
