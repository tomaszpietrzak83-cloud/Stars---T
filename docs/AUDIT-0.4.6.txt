TOMEK-STARS 0.4.6 — AUTOMATION, STATIONS AND STATISTICS

Gameplay changes
- Direct, zero-donation fuel-network cruises no longer reserve combat/bomber
  missions or contact-recon scouts permanently. Actual tanker commitments stay.
- Patrols reconsider local interceptions yearly. Economical repositioning and
  shared moving rallies reserve a real return. Low tank percentage alone does
  not veto a funded nearby kill; unfunded patrols still return for fuel.
- Surplus bomber cover is detached and its original roles restored. Bombers
  stay pooled, avoiding the previous cycle of one escort per individual bomber.
  Unknown orbital weapons / insufficient stealth-warning coverage can retain
  minimal cover. This does not assert an unseen enemy is harmless.
- Priority colony reconnaissance precedes spare incoming-fleet reconnaissance;
  an active bombing target ranks first among eligible stale priority colonies.
  Copied 2477 proof: one scout is routed to Covenant Reach, whose report was
  42 years old; old population estimates are not accepted as current facts.
- Tanker and customer can both approach a common, funded meeting point. Prefer
  both free engine speeds within a bounded 20-turn horizon; otherwise use a
  funded economical meeting. A full customer is released. Revalidate requests
  resolved by an earlier donor, preventing a nil-request crash.
- Radiating automatic freighters reject new passengers and invasions, unload
  legacy passengers at a friendly colony, and keep mineral/infrastructure/
  salvage work. Separate radiating escorts do not irradiate a passenger carrier.
- Predict a forthcoming bombardment using published defenses and own arriving
  bombs, without RNG calls or hidden enemy populations. Full transports may
  stage outside the hostile orbit. They cannot invade on a forecast: a real
  refreshed report unlocks the landing. Nearby loaded landings let bombers
  move onward once softening is no longer needed. Stale dense reports need
  reconnaissance first; orbital bases and remaining defenses block this plan.
- Trade convoys prefer pure Large Freighter hulls. Before sacrificing smaller
  carriers, inspect actual next-year production of automatic freighter
  replacements. Manual/composition orders are not reassigned; no ships are
  ordered implicitly. Queue forecasts preserve state and game RNG.
- Spare sweep-capable patrols clear local hostile minefields affecting colonies
  or active routes, entering at safe warp from the rim with funded return.
  Native sweep capacity is used, including Gatling weapons; our/friendly and
  detonating fields are excluded. Local interception retains precedence.

Starbases
- Upgrade creates a same-hull design family; Rebuild family queues its latest
  version on eligible owned planets. Existing unrelated base orders are kept,
  duplicate rebuilds are disabled, old projects disappear from build choices.
- An ongoing family station order follows the newest successor and preserves
  allocated resources while mineral-blocked. Existing station remains active
  until native replacement completes. Fleet-number and planet-number spaces
  are not conflated through the mobile-fleet rebuild flags.
- Native StarbaseUpgradeCost already reuses shared components and applies
  conversion discounts, including 50% credit for a changed old hull. There is
  no second scrap credit for the same parts. Existing native pricing is kept.

Statistics and battle reports
- Statistics tab persists event-based counters and up to 200 yearly rows:
  actual kill/loss counts and dry tonnage, construction/scrapping/installations,
  production allocations/refunds/research, mining and recovered debris.
- Existing saves clearly state when detailed accounting starts. Historical
  production counters remain; old expenditure/kills are not fabricated.
- Battle report columns and message links show destroyed count plus kT for
  each side, retaining red/green colors. Battle tonnage uses per-ship mass
  captured in the record (including its cargo); incomplete old reports show
  unavailable instead of fabricated zero. Partial destroyed stacks are counted.

Performance
Astra's isolated 0.4.5 audit: median native generation 60.909853 -> 45.248634 s
(25.71% reduction), three runs per variant with matching normalized complete
state. See docs/ASTRA-PERFORMANCE-REPORT.txt for proof and limitations.
Integrated the small sort-local approaching-colony cache and incremental
single-tail fleet-owner index. Existing newer patrol logic is retained.
Independent comparator oracle and owner-index regressions pass. These historical
0.4.5 timings are not presented as benchmarks of the newer gameplay changes.

Additional economy and defense requirements (2026-11-02)
- Defending against landings: reported freighter/colonizer hulls heading toward
  owned colonies receive first interception priority. Other local transports
  rank ahead of scouts; immediate bomber defense and force assessment remain.
  Existing pursuits reconsider nearby landing threats from public reports.
- Each functioning shipyard keeps a 300-kT floor of each mineral, or its larger
  near-term manufacturing demand. Real blocked orders precede passenger work;
  idle stock replenishment follows passenger service.
- A current 62-kT shortage does not cap a shipment: full 210-kT useful mineral
  loads can build the yard's future stock. Dedicated supply fills the mineral
  buffer before incidental cargo. Other incoming loads are accounted for.
- Quantity reservations replace exclusive source/yard locks; several full
  freighters can serve a large deficiency. Empty stale underfilled pickups are
  re-planned, without disabling the role. Pickup and loaded delivery are both
  budgeted with actual fuel and a return, preserving the full-hold requirement.
- Mineral source candidates are owned worlds and actual unowned mining sites;
  avoid repeatedly scanning every unowned planet for every yard and freighter.
- Read-only compact copy of current personal year 2483: MF32 waits at Petra
  despite 4836 kT ironium; old job requests 62 of 210 kT. The copied-state test
  proves a complete 210-kT pickup while preserving source stock/accounting.
