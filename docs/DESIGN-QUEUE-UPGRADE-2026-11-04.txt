Designer upgrades update production immediately (2026-11-04)

Creating an upgraded version replaces all queued ship/starbase items from older
members of its family on this player's planets. A regular copy/create is not
an upgrade. Quantities, order, paid allocation, notes and reserved metadata stay
intact. Existing physical fleets and bases are not changed by saving a design.
Design, player orders and changed planets are saved in a single transaction.
Installed bases are loaded to recompute dock/upgrade costs and queue estimates.
The response supplies changed planets to the browser and WASM universe.
