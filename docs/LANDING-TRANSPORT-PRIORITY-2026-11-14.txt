Bounded landings and civilian transport priority
================================================

User requirement
----------------
Transporters primarily deliver colonists, minerals, factories, mines and
defenses. Clear the orbital base, bombard the colony, then land a replaceable
army. Reserve 200,000-400,000 people per target, not millions.

Behavior
--------
Civilian logistics assigns work before recruitment of new landing transports,
including when an older save enabled LandingsFirst. Existing civilian jobs and
packed installation cargo remain ineligible for landing recruitment.

A new army is only assembled for a reported base-free, softened target:
at most 100,000 reported defenders with no reported ground defenses, or the
existing percentage-bombing handoff threshold. Reconnaissance, orbital combat,
route and fuel checks remain mandatory. An already weak colony does not need
an artificial extra bombing round. Departure repeats the readiness check.

Army requirements are bounded to 200,000-400,000 people. Recruitment counts
loaded passengers and reserved pickups together; full small holds may round
the requested amount upward but may not exceed 400,000. Large empty transport
groups split into useful civilian-sized holds before recruitment. Pickup loads
respect their reservation after a capacity change.

Existing oversized armies and armies tied to bases or unsoftened colonies are
released before civilian planning. The native split operation preserves real
ships, cargo and fuel. Troops receive ordinary passenger deliveries according
to colony demand and route availability; release does not teleport them home.
Requests for orbital clearance and bombing live on player reports, without
reserving transports. Bombers prefer these cleared objectives. Coordinated
landing departures are recognized by bomber handoff.

Four years without a change of leader position, loaded population or operation
phase release a stalled army, with five years before recruitment for the same
target. Old stationary saved operations are audited immediately using their
recorded start year. These safeguards do not bypass military/fuel checks.

Evidence
--------
Copied campaign input: game 21, Embers Beyond Eridanus, year 2500.
The initial release test reduced reserved people from 9,660,000 to 253,000,
returning 9,407,000 to civilian duties. All 2,652 own ships and 2,613,442 units
of fleet fuel, and all onboard population, were preserved by release/splitting.
Subsequent logistics planning produced four operations, all within the cap,
and civilian routes carrying 980,700 people. This is planned movement, not a
claim of completed passenger deliveries. The personal saved game was not
advanced. Private evidence: ../audit-results/landing-bounded-copy.log.

Focused native campaigns verify base destruction, actual bombing and capture,
including a bomber pending retirement. Regression checks cover oversized army
release/conservation, no recruitment before bombardment, capped reservations,
and stalled-army release with a recruitment cooldown.

Build, full-suite and final-copy verification are recorded separately in
SUGGESTIONS-CHECKLIST.txt and ../audit-results/landing-bounded-*.log.
