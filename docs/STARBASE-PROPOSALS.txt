Late starbase proposals - implemented in 0.3.0; audited against native hull capacities

EEE Bastion Station (all races)
Research: Construction 22, Energy 20, Electronics 16.
Hull cost: 160 ironium / 110 boranium / 380 germanium / 850 resources.
Unlimited dock; armor 1250; initiative 17; range bonus 1; repair bonus 0.15.
Six weapon batteries of 20 (120 total); two shield banks of 24; two
shield/armor banks of 24; four electrical slots of 4; two orbital slots of 1.
AR population limit 2 million: it must not supersede the AR Death Star.
Versus ISB Ultra Station: 25% more weapon capacity and armor, available
later without ISB. ISB retains its normal discount and earlier availability.
Use a symmetric cross with the dock in the centre and matching batteries
at opposite ends. No free gate, mass driver or extra weapon range.

EEE Sanctuary Star (Alternate Reality only)
Research: Construction 24, Energy 23, Electronics 20.
Hull cost: 160 ironium / 110 boranium / 460 germanium / 1050 resources.
Unlimited dock; armor 1875; initiative 19; range bonus 1; repair bonus 0.15.
Population limit 3.75 million versus Death Star's 3 million (+25%).
Keep the Death Star's four separate 32-weapon batteries (128 total).
Shield banks 36+36; shield/armor banks 24+24; six electrical banks of 4;
two orbital slots of 1. Keep the 0.5 innate penetrating-scanner multiplier.
Prioritize population, resilience and a clear symmetric station silhouette,
rather than combining every offensive bonus in a universal best hull.

Balance validation required before implementation:
Same-cost station builds using identical tech; fleet-versus-station battles;
AR population/resource scaling and ISB/US costs; no slot overlaps; destruction
must retain native AR population rules. These are proposals, not live content.
