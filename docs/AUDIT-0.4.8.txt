Resource header
Beside Submit Turn, the header sums gross annual resources from owned planets.
Research is blue and links to the research screen. Its total uses the same
formula as native ComputePlayerResearchSpec: reserved research plus estimated
production leftovers. These are annual forecasts, not historical spending.
Foreign planet reports are excluded. Counts update with the universe data.

Maintenance messages
One summary per player and generated turn replaces automatic maintenance's
individual scrap/build/progress/completion messages. It includes actual old
ships scrapped by design, recovered minerals/resources, actual successor ships
built by design and completed fleet rebuilds. Partial construction is not
reported as finished, and next-year reports do not repeat previous events.
The original 70% regrouping threshold is retained; groups completed in the same
turn do not also appear in the partial-progress count. Manual scrapping and
ordinary production messages retain their existing behavior.
