Previously open requirements addressed in this revision
177: Passenger round-trip preflight retains the actual delivery destination,
so a fully unloaded return is not rejected as though the cargo were still full.
Full useful loads outrank source-growth waits. An empty waiting transporter
can switch to another currently full source; existing cargo is not discarded.
178: Rebuilds can disperse inside an owned fuel-base sector rather than first
visiting the exact rally coordinate. Native yard reservations persist, include
incoming workload and resolve to the latest family design. Actual incoming
scrap/rebuild assignments create mineral demand before the replacement queue
exists; source production remains protected and scrap mineral credit is counted.
179: Planet priorities influence nearby base-sector patrol/escort coverage in
addition to the existing bombardment, landing, reinforcement and scout refresh
controllers. Actual funded routes and combat-strength checks remain mandatory.
182: Radiating escorts accompany passenger transports as separate fleets;
their engines do not contaminate the passenger fleet's native radiation flag.
197: Fresh mixed bomber formations visibly approaching an owned planet have
a narrow observed siege corridor, rather than a broad interception circle.
Their actual estimated firepower remains relevant to planetary defense.
213: Patrol leaders reconsider fresh targets yearly using public predicted
endpoints and local owned-territory opportunities. Nearby assigned hunters can
join one physical group against multi-ship unarmed targets. Moving away at warp
9 is not assumed interceptable from behind. Native gate mass/cargo/range limits
are checked; gate waypoints retain a planetary target through departure checks.
214: Mystery Trader priority persists through the API/UI. A one-shot convoy
reserves real surplus minerals and available automatic freighters, loads,
physically merges, stages on the reported course, and uses the native meeting.
The whole offering fleet is absorbed. Insufficient cargo capacity/stock does
not reserve a partial offering or borrow manual ships. Cancelling priority or
losing the trader releases the reservation. Unexpected course changes cannot
silently extend the final interception beyond its funded movement budget.
215: Idle patrol/escort coverage uses stable fuel-base sectors, short economical
local loops, local contacts and uncovered/prioritized bases. It no longer scores
destinations higher simply because they are farther from the homeworld.
216: Complete local archive of 175 user-role messages, including corrections.
