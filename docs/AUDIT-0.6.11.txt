tomek-stars 0.6.11 - local patrol packs, bomber protection and design Auto orders
2026-11-04

Behavior
- Native combat forecasts no longer reject a useful victory merely because
  unarmed auxiliaries survive. Bombers must be eliminated and at least half
  the modeled combat capability removed; an unarmed-only interception must
  destroy at least one enemy. Survivor damage and repair downtime stay separate.
- Every sampled outcome must lose at most 5% of both own ship count and weighted
  replacement value. Explicit player fleet/design battle plans remain binding.
  Unknown encounters of eight or more reported ships still require scouting;
  the existing conservative small-unknown-contact permission is preserved.
- Reconcile reservations before filtering unavailable leaders. Refit/pending
  refit releases or transfers command without rewriting the refit order. Check
  leader identity, target and operational members; retain merged retreat stacks.
- Physically co-located sufficient subsets merge through native orders, recheck
  the exact result and launch, even when the previous commander is elsewhere.
  Useful late support follows without vetoing launch; excess returns to work.
  Reinforcement candidates are ordered by actual funded gathering ETA/condition.
- Recon gathering is bounded to a fraction of available ships (up to 32);
  repeated missing scans do not grow reservations without limit. A gathering
  ship may take one funded local kill when it still meets the existing rally
  deadline. No detours for imminent colony defense or repeated deadline resets.
- Production slow-prey screening uses the same patrol controller instead of
  locking combat units under an independent containment tag. Observer selection
  scales with operational capacity up to eight targets. Existing real-debris,
  defensive collector, pickup/delivery, fuel and lost-contact rules remain.
- Bombardment protection evaluates actually funded one-turn DD arrivals with
  separate native fleet formations. Colony defense/assemblies/refit stay
  protected. A separate deterministic 2048-work defense reserve cannot be spent
  by the 4096-work offensive planner. Neither budget authorizes unsafe combat.
- One-year advance withdrawal applies to remote bombardment (outside 150 ly of
  current own colonies). Warning uses public engine components and reported
  motion, not hidden fuel/orders. Local groups seek DD protection first; normal
  imminent-danger safety still applies. Escape favors the highest funded safe
  warp and rejects endpoints interceptable by another reported stationary pack.
  Per the user's final clarification, a fresh stationary bomber formation over
  an own colony remains committed to its siege: its occupied orbit is dangerous,
  but no hypothetical maximum-speed pursuit is invented. Reported departure
  immediately restores the mobile interception envelope.
- Designs expose Auto orders in creation/editing and on in-use blueprints.
  New ships get the role at construction; successors inherit it. Changing the
  default does not alter existing ships, components, creation year or orders.
  Replacements preserve their actual prior manual/automatic role and mission.
  Equipment validation and persistence use the existing JSON spec: no migration.

Limits
- Forecast samples estimate risk; new enemy orders/reinforcements can change a
  real battle. Reassessment at departure/pursuit remains mandatory. Small
  unknown contacts use the explicitly retained strength rule, not an invented
  native equipment forecast. No safe route or reinforcement is invented when
  fuel, timing, missing equipment or observed threat envelopes cannot confirm it.
- Pending refit and other protected duties reduce actual available DD numbers;
  total empire destroyer count alone cannot certify a local defense.
