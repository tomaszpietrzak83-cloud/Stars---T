Implemented user clarification, superseding recommendations in the earlier audit:
- New colony assignments: maximum 25 years, funded by onboard fuel and known fixed docks. No assistedColonyTarget fallback and no tanker acceleration dependency when selecting a new target.

- Reserve targets only for real colony voyages; safety/refit/idle tags alone do not reserve planets.
- Defer maintenance while a colony delivery, landing or retirement is active. Recover loaded legacy colonizers from unstarted refit returns without undoing already committed replacements.
