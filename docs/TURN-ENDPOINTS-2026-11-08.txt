Implementation
- All route roles assess reported enemy predicted arrivals, retaining existing3ly
  report tolerance, fresh-report/diplomacy filters and hostile-base checks.
- Check the next annual stop as well as the intended destination; use native
  sub-light-year arrival snapping. Reconsider routes yearly from public reports.
- Cache arrival threat independently of departure point within existing scopes.
- Avoidance/escape no longer treats crossing paths as combat or invents a pursuit
  by each stationary enemy toward every possible escape point.
- Preserve patrol attacks confirmed by the native combat evaluator instead of
  cancelling them with the simpler emergency power ratio.
- Mine avoidance, fuel reserves, landing clearance and mandatory escorts remain.
- Existing uncommitted server/shipproduction.go and its test were preserved.
- Gate-aware optimistic travel-time lower bound rejects impossible deadlines
  before expensive fuel-reserve searches. It allows onboard gates, mixed fleets,
  appropriate entry/exit mass limits and direct flight. It ignores fuel/mines and
  gate connectivity constraints to avoid overestimating minimum travel time.
