Version 0.6.0 — debris coordination, destruction priorities and report zoom
Date: 2026-11-03

Evidence and behaviour
- A consistent online backup of the user's 2444 database was inspected read-only.
  Known debris #3 contained 5 kT ironium and 1 kT germanium. Several transports
  had speculative battle reservations; these excluded them from actual debris
  selection and did not track a moving hunter's updated interception point.
- Actual known debris can now preempt an empty speculative collector. Existing
  logistics pickups, loaded deliveries, safety, repair, assembly and rebuild
  work remain protected. The copied-2444 regression assigns Small Freighter #8
  to known debris #3, without changing the year or saving the scratch state.
- A speculative collection stores hunter and enemy identities, updates the
  predicted battle point, and releases cancelled, obsolete or unreachable work.
  Both the superior patrol and collector must have a funded route reaching the
  battle this movement phase. No battle is delayed waiting for a distant cargo
  ship. Real enemy orders, cargo and hidden positions are not consulted.
- A collector physically present discovers debris without scanner equipment.
  It can load at waypoint zero of the following turn, before annual decay.
  No instant BattleSalvage setting is required for this normal collection.
- Deep-space decay is now 8% or at least 8 kT per mineral/year, versus 10%/10 kT.
  Deposits on planets remain ordinary planetary minerals and do not decay.
  Small unattended debris can still disappear; timely collection matters.
- Fleet crosshairs store playerNum:fleetNum identities, so equal fleet numbers
  belonging to different races are independent. Patrols prioritize these orders
  and can pursue beyond their ordinary 150 ly opportunity radius with sufficient
  strength, fuel and a funded return. Existing nearby groups can retarget.
  Combat ships are kept available for fresh explicit priority contacts instead
  of being borrowed for small debris pickups. Strength/safety checks still apply.
- A received native battle report, including an allied shared report, clears
  a priority only when its actual target fleet has been destroyed. Loss of scan
  contact or a surviving enemy does not complete the order. Planet orders clear
  when the marked planet becomes owned by the ordering player.
- Report navigation updates the stored map center while retaining its scale.
  Homeworld/default navigation can still preserve the entire saved viewport.
