Required operational design:
1. Reconcile availability and assignment ownership before reserving combat units.
   On leader refit/unavailability, transfer command to a real eligible survivor
   or release the reservation. Remove invalid members from readiness and ETA.
   Keep manual refit requests intact. Handle split/merge/death/number reuse.
2. Rank urgent threats, then choose local forces by funded route ETA, composition,
   condition and battle forecast. Separate ready local group from en-route support.
   The common target has a deadline/rendezvous; a distant member cannot veto launch.
3. Define mission success using dangerous survivors: stop bombing, defeat or
   sufficiently neutralize combatants under the 5% own-loss constraint. An escaped
   unarmed auxiliary or scout must not veto a useful defensive victory. Avoid
   approving a mere harmless skirmish that leaves bombardment essentially intact.
4. When the co-located subset already satisfies the mission forecast, merge it
   through native orders, recompute exact fleet specs/fuel/plan, validate departure
   and bounded pursuit, then attack. Treat late units as reinforcement/follow-up.
5. Permit bounded local cleanup while gathering: actual local target forecast,
   route fuel and threat checks, updated return-to-rendezvous ETA, main objective
   deadline. Recompute after any battle. No repeated diversion that resets the
   main gathering clock; never independently feed fragments to the main enemy.
6. Provision useful surplus in parallel with recon. Stop growing a reservation
   merely because a missing scan or an escaping auxiliary prevents Ready=true.
   After fresh intelligence retain useful damage reduction or release excess.
7. Preserve successful pack across follow-up turns. Refresh reports, damage,
   fuel and actual course-directed reinforcements. Do not assume another encounter
   is guaranteed next turn. Apply scouts and collector logistics to the mission.
8. Integrate slow-prey observation/containment with the same active local swarm
   and mission queue, so another containment tag does not immobilize a fighter.
   Existing slowPreyTargets truncates to two targets per player: review this
   bounded concurrency against actual ready combat/scout/collector resources.
   Do not remove all bounds or divert urgent defenders merely for salvage.
   Cargo capacity, survivable combat presence, real pickup/delivery and fuel
   routes are part of the mission. Planet-orbit debris already goes to planet.
