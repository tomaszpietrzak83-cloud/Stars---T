Combined source
- Merge 199f7121 combines reviewed recapture commit 772462ff with main 5ee57b3b.
  Designer upgrades still replace older-family queued ship/base projects at once,
  retaining quantity, paid allocation, order, notes and one-off Auto orders.

  scout hulls accept the deliberate contact risk; actual scanner evidence is
  transmitted before combat. Incoming-colony fleet reconnaissance stays protected.
- Known orbital bases get a separate, bounded assault of physically available
  local ships. Full reported equipment, the whole observed encounter, native
  forecasts, manual plans, fuel and the 5% count/value/mass limits remain binding.
  Forecasts must destroy the base. New unsafe defenders release the claim so
  actual local reinforcements can merge and restore a safe assault.

  Pending retirement/refit retains the mission throughout multiple bombing turns;
  actual capture then releases it to maintenance. Ground estimates use the
  strongest public-rule defense bonus, without reading a private enemy race.

  current habitability or immediate instaforming, never future construction.
- Loaded accepted invasions keep destination-specific clearance as reports age.
  Changed target/owner/mission and fresh hostile base/defense or population above
  100,000 invalidate it. Real native landing, current route, escort, full-load
  and source population checks remain. Pending landings stage at reachable owned
  colonies. No hidden intelligence or forced unsafe combat is introduced.
- The global production panel now contains its table within narrow screens.
