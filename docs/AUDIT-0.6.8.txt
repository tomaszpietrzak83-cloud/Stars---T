Version 0.6.8 — combined resources, station and mineral-stock map

Request
- In R view, also show whether a planet has a station and its minerals, with
  a readable combined presentation.

Presentation
- Keep the sky-blue annual-production circle and its empire-relative AREA scale
  from 0.6.7. A station has its own outlined silhouette above the circle; actual
  stations and forts retain their distinct existing colors. Zero-production
  colonies still show their planet, station and inventory.
- Three separate vertical bars on a dark backing sit to the right of the planet.
  Bright blue I = Ironium, green B = Boranium, yellow G = Germanium. These are
  surface stocks in kT, not mineral concentrations or annual production.
- All stock bars use the same mineralScale already available in the planet's
  mineral graph. A positive stock has a minimum one-pixel mark; an empty stock
  has only its dark track. A plus marks a stock above the shared scale.
- An on-map legend explains annual production, station position, I/B/G and the
  full-bar kT scale. It responds when the mineral scale changes, wraps on phones,
  and yields to the distance-measurement strip while measuring.
- Owned planets use their current station/cargo specs. Known foreign surface
  stocks may be shown; absent enemy-stock knowledge does not create invented
  bars. Other planets retain normal known station markers.
- Station and inventory stay outside the production disc. Labels, fleet counts,
  selection arrows, waypoint clearance and edge culling include the overlay.
