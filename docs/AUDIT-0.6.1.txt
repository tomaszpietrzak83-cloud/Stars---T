Version 0.6.1 — rapid starbase upgrades and design creation years
Date: 2026-11-03

Behaviour
- Starbases previously used native component-reuse pricing only. Rapid mobile
  ship recovery explicitly excluded them. They now share the direct-successor,
  same-family/same-hull rule with an inclusive four-game-year creation gap.
- The rapid alternative is the full normal successor blueprint cost minus 90%
  of the old blueprint's current normal cost, rounded down per resource. Costs
  are calculated with the player's actual miniaturization and racial modifiers.
- For each mineral and resources, choose the lower of native component-reuse
  pricing and the rapid alternative, clamped to zero. These refunds are not
  added together, and surplus credit is not minted into stock. Existing native
  reuse discounts can therefore never become more expensive under this rule.
- Native production/forecasts, AI estimates and the WASM UI calculator use the
  same player-aware method. The old base stays operational until the new base
  is fully funded and completes normally. A first base is still full price.
- Legacy designs with unknown creation years, skipped versions, unrelated
  families, foreign/transferred designs and different hulls retain normal pricing.
  The date gap concerns blueprint creation, not the later construction year.
- Ship Designs cards and saved-design details display the creation game year
  for both ships and bases. Missing legacy dates display "unknown" rather than
  guessing a year from a real-world database timestamp.
- Creation year and predecessor are exposed in ShipDesignSpec protobuf fields
  68/69 for display and WASM forecasts. Native creation/edit endpoints still own
  these values: copied or forged client history cannot alter them.
