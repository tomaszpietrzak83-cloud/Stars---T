TRANSPORT BEHAVIOR
Civilian population/mineral work claims available ships before new landing recruitment by default; already committed landing operations remain coordinated. Shared outgoing reservations prevent civilian and military planners booking the same colonists. Mines/factories/defenses use existing routes and real cargo capacity; installation loading is opportunistic, with factory/mine reserve computed from operating population after departure. Defense export leaves80 absolute units even if maximum defenses is200.
Mineral demand already accounts for queued future production (three-year yard resource budget plus the next unit), source reserve, incoming bookings and useful demand at arrival. A full useful shipment is preferred; unavailable useful cargo, salvage collection, or remaining destination capacity can prevent every observed hold being full. No claim that every fleet at every instant is full.
Spare freighters load armies, physically merge one annual jump from target and assemble scout/patrol support. Reconnaissance checks orbit; enemy base blocks landing pending assessed patrol destruction and renewed reconnaissance. Incoming warships require cover already on station. Large useful attrition drops can weaken defenders without capture; minimum expected damage prevents waste against overwhelming ground defense. An army blocked by an unavailable base assault can retarget a nearby reported base-free colony, retaining cargo and requiring new reconnaissance. Empty completed armies split into usable civilian holds again.
Safety is based on actual combat mechanics: fleets sharing the same final coordinates can battle even without a planet (cs/turn.go fleetBattle). Merely crossing flight paths does not itself trigger that battle. Hence planet-only immunity is not guaranteed; endpoint checks remain appropriate.
