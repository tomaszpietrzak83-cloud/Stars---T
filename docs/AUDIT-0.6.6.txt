Reported loss / read-only diagnosis

  destroyers assigned to enemy 12:25 (three transports). The transports' public
  course approaches stationary 12:32: 25 ships at (1694,149), including eighteen
  armed destroyers. The year-2460 battle there contains 28 enemy ships; all five
  own ships die and no enemy ship dies. Private archives stay excluded from Git.
- A 30-ly neighborhood misses the stationary formation 36 ly from the transport.
  Relative public motion and the bounded pursuit endpoint must both be checked.

Implementation
- Production patrol orders now evaluate deep, isolated native battle copies
  constructed from owned fleets and the player's discovered design reports.
  Enemy private design pointers, race, technology, cargo, fuel and orders are
  not used. Every sample owns mutable fleet/token copies and its own seeded RNG.
- Test mixed merged formations together with automatic/default, damage, range
  and defensive own tactics; preserve explicit fleet and design-level player
  plans. Enemy hypotheses include five explicit tactics and native per-design
  automatic tactics. Automatic plan ownership restores the previous plan when
  a mission ends or manual control resumes, retaining later player edits.
- Reject sampled wipes, surviving enemies and excessive mean own lost value.
  Compare destroyed value, damaged-survivor value and stopped/orbital repair
  readiness. Useful reinforcements can be retained to reduce damage; surplus
  whole groups can be released. Existing small-unarmed-stack splitting remains.
- Cache exact combat inputs for the orders phase. Screen cheaply, then confirm
  with 16/32/64 seeds according to observed damage/loss. A deterministic 4096
  work-unit budget per player fails closed. Sampling is not a victory guarantee.
- Public relative trajectories and contact time include stationary formations
  approached by moving transports. Nearby receding traffic does not count merely
  because it lies inside 30 ly. Hostile starbases require a dedicated assault.
  Revalidate the final departure and every native bounded pursuit endpoint.
- Reconnaissance of uncertain groups of eight or more remains mandatory; smaller
  unknown contacts remain eligible under a conservative reported-strength bound.
  Unknown equipment is never presented as a native equipment simulation.
- Scout, combat, search and collection share public mission priorities. Already
  bombing colonies and incoming threats have urgency/deadlines. Equipment jobs
  deduplicate shared unknown designs; urgent fleet contact precedes planet refresh.
  Known contacts covered by owned sensors need no extra slow-formation watcher.
- Funded surplus gathering proceeds alongside reconnaissance, outside reported
  reach. Known-force gathering evaluates funded rendezvous plus merged-fleet
  travel, rather than always using the arithmetic mean or a two-year timeout.
  Existing useful free movement, radiating warp 6 and frontier advance remain.
- Server-owned lost slow-contact memory persists in automation JSON and is
  protected from ordinary client order replacement. Searches expand uncertainty
  and require funded safe scanner routes. Fresh reports are required for attacks;
  number disappearance/reuse is not proof of destruction or zero fuel.
- Collectors stage ahead, retain defensive plans and transfer reservations to
  actual known fields. Pickup under a collector precedes empty return routing
  and escort waiting. Debris identity includes position; reused numbers cannot
  load a remote field. Real delivery, fuel rescue and maintenance stay protected.

Limits
- Search/selection is bounded, not a global optimal fleet composition proof.
  Hidden changes and unobserved reinforcements remain uncertain. Larger known
  forces must be gathered when a forecast refuses the present force.
- Three collectors cannot guarantee recovery of every rapidly decaying split
  field; 32 kT delivered is evidence of real pickup, not full salvage recovery.
  No personal game is simulated or advanced to produce these campaign results.
