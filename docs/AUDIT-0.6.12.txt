tomek-stars 0.6.12 - minesweepers, refit safety and clearer map/combat feedback
2026-11-04

Implemented requests 275-279

1. Gatling-only human designs are dedicated minesweepers, identified by equipment,
   not the design name. New construction defaults to Minesweeper; explicit Manual
   remains available. Automatic patrol, escort and combat assignments cannot borrow
   these ships. Native battle stacks use Disengage with no firing targets even if
   intercepted under an aggressive fleet plan. Enemy equipment does not reveal its
   private automation: report-only combat models retain armed enemy Gatling ships.
   Mine approach separates economical cruise from safe-speed entry, then follows
   the shrinking field toward its center. Actual fuel/return reserves are required.

2. Refit and replacement rendezvous no longer bypass emergency civilian safety.
   Safe rally selection updates the group's server-owned queue metadata without
   canceling replacement quantities, paid allocations or saved original orders.
   Unsafe civilian yards are excluded; scrapping pauses before a reported raid.
   Vulnerable same-turn construction is deferred because production follows the
   movement phase, leaving newly built ships unable to evacuate before battle.
   Warships and other queue entries can still build. Safe refits continue normally
   instead of being diverted to ordinary repairs for ships about to be replaced.
   Forecasts count nearby fleets actually staying and the owned base, with native
   separate formations. Stations cannot dilute mobile loss allowances. Mobile
   count, value and mass loss limits are 5%, own lost mass cannot exceed enemy mass
   destroyed, and a defensive forecast cannot accept any civilian ship loss.
   Existing remote-only advance withdrawal and stationary-siege exceptions remain.

3. A separate Fleet routes icon on desktop and phone controls own/selected/dragged
   fleet waypoints and reported enemy fleet course lines. Planet ship routing lines
   follow this control. Packets/traders and actual navigation orders are unchanged.
   The setting is persisted; older settings default to visible.

4. Production queue text and selected row backgrounds are green when at least one
   unit completes this turn, including a multi-year batch. The first completion in
   later years is blue. Skipped/impossible estimates retain their distinct text.

5. Battle attack feedback adds a yellow destroyed-ship count with its own ship
   marker beside armor and shield damage. It uses native tokensDestroyed for the
   displayed attack, aggregates shots per square, and omits zero. Phase zero and
   moves do not display stale losses; normal playback still controls visibility.
