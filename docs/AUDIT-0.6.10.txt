Change
- Build per-player fleet-report lookup maps for the scan phase, including all
  ally recipients before scanning starts. Append discoveries to both the list
  and lookup; existing reports are updated through the same pointers.
- Dispose of lookup maps when scanning exits, including error paths. Transient
  report clearing also invalidates the map. Other phases keep existing lookup
  semantics; no persistent cache or hidden-information access is introduced.
- Update the existing ship-design report index incrementally on discovery,
  avoiding a full rebuild after each appended design.
- No scanning ranges, diplomacy, combat decisions, RNG consumption, report
  ordering or transport rules are intentionally changed.
