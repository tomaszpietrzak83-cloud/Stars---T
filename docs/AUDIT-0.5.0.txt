0.5.0: consecutive-upgrade recycling, 2026-11-02

Request 250
The final requirement supersedes the initial proposal to charge 10% of the new
ship's price. A qualifying automatic rebuild instead scraps the old ship for
90% of its own current design cost, including resources and all three minerals.
The successor is queued at its full normal construction price. Cargo is still
unloaded normally and is not part of the 90% recycling calculation.

Eligibility
- Owned, non-transferred ship designs on the same hull and in the same family.
- The physical old ship must use the direct preceding version of the successor.
  Jumping over an intermediate version does not qualify.
- Known project creation years must differ by 0-4 game years, inclusive.
- The interval refers to project dates; finishing a delivery or returning to a
  shipyard later does not invalidate the qualifying pair.
- This special recovery is exactly 90%, rounded down per resource for each
  scrapped quantity. Ordinary racial/EEE recycling bonuses are not added again.
- Manual scrapping, retirement without replacement, unrelated designs and
  starbase replacements keep their existing rules.

Example: old ship R100/I40/B20/G10, new ship R120/I50/B25/G15.
Recovery is R90/I36/B18/G9; full-price construction leaves a net requirement
of R30/I14/B7/G6. It is not 10% of the successor's price.

Persistence and existing games
The server now records project creation year and direct predecessor in existing
design-spec JSON. These are server-owned history fields, preserved by spec
recomputation and allowed blueprint edits. New games also date starter designs.
No database schema migration is needed. Older saved designs without a creation
year retain normal recovery; their historical dates are not guessed. Creating
new dated versions establishes history for subsequent rapid upgrades.
The newest version records the actual latest family predecessor even when the
user starts editing from an older family member.

Planning
Actual scrapping credits the real shipyard stock once. Replacement queue items
retain their normal unit cost and do not receive a second paid allocation.
Yard distribution, incoming rebuild workload and mineral-supply reservations
use the qualifying credit. Task-completion and funded-return precedence remain
unchanged.
