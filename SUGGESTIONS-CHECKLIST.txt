- A complete Python rewrite was not requested after choosing the Go/Svelte fork.

- Search is bounded at 20,000 states; assisted colony fallback evaluates 32 promising targets.
- Non-one-way mission endpoints require a funded return to an actual owned fuel dock.
- Unknown enemies/future orders cannot be predicted. No future tanker fuel is invented.
- No reachable service means wait/request rescue, not travel without fuel.
- Eight palette variants share each hull/station's original new geometry; they are not eight different hull silhouettes.
